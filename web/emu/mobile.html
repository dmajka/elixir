ELIXIR@VERSION@

The FM-1 running Elixir, in your browser. Turn your phone sideways for a wider layout. Saves and kits stay in this browser.

Kits

Bring LXR-02 kits (.SND files) in. They stay in this browser, with your saved projects, until you clear its data. On an iPhone, pick several .SND files in Files, or a .zip of a kit folder or a whole SD card.

Pick .SND or .zip

Turn PRESETS for the kit browser: the LXR-02's 85 factory kits (F1 …), then the library (L1 …, with Hrtl's DLC and UNTZ kits in 97–128), then the slots. KNOB 1 jumps between them, OCT+ loads one. SAVE → PROJECT keeps kits loaded into slots with a project.

(.ktlb: the library page puts it on an FM-1)

Restore a kit library

Playing it

Drag a knob up or down, or drag a parameter at the bottom of the screen to turn the knob below it. The black keys are the FM-1's: D1 … OH select a track, 1–4 show a bar. OCT− is SHIFT, OCT+ is COPY / OK; hold one with a finger and press with another for combinations.
