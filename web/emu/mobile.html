ELIXIR@VERSION@

The FM-1 running Elixir, in your browser. Turn your phone sideways for a wider layout. Saves and kits stay in this browser.

Kits

Bring LXR-02 kits (.SND files) in. They stay in this browser, with your saved projects, until you clear its data. On an iPhone, pick several .SND files in Files, or a .zip of a kit folder or a whole SD card.

Pick .SND or .zip

Then turn PRESETS for the kit browser, KNOB 1 to switch between the slots and the library, and OCT+ to load one. SAVE → PROJECT keeps kits loaded into slots with a project.

(.ktlb: the library page puts it on an FM-1)

Restore a kit library

Playing it

Drag a knob up or down. The black keys are the FM-1's: D1 … OH select a track, 1–4 show a bar. OCT− is SHIFT, OCT+ is COPY / OK; hold one with a finger and press with another for combinations.
