Elixir library

Back up the projects and kits on your FM-1, put them back, move projects between slots, and bring in kits and projects from an LXR-02.

You need Chrome or Edge (for Web MIDI), and Elixir on the FM-1 (or Kettle 0.7 or later, its former name) connected with a USB data cable. Close other apps that use MIDI, and allow MIDI (SysEx) when the browser asks.

Projects

The FM-1's four project slots and the session (what AUTOSAVE keeps). A dot marks the project that's playing.

SlotProject
  • Download saves the slot's project to this computer as a .ktp file.
  • Upload puts a .ktp file (or one project from a backup) into the slot. What was there stays until the upload is complete, so an interrupted upload loses nothing.
  • Save here saves what's playing on the FM-1 into the slot, as SAVE → PROJECT does.
  • Load loads the slot on the FM-1, replacing what's playing.

Back up & restore

Back up everything

Saves every project, and the session, to one .ktbk file. Do this before installing other firmware (V15 or another): Elixir's own updates keep your projects, other firmware may not. The kit library has a backup of its own.

Restore a backup

Puts every project in a .ktbk file back into the slot it came from. To put one project into a different slot, use its slot's Upload above.

Kits: slots or library?

Elixir keeps your kits in two places in the FM-1's flash. A third set, the LXR-02's 85 factory kits, is built into Elixir itself (from 0.19, with Erica Synths' permission): it takes none of this space and is always there.

  • The kit slots (64) belong to the project, as on the LXR-02. A pattern names its kit by slot number (a pattern whose Voices are KIT loads its kit), and the slots are saved with the project (SAVE → PROJECT, or AUTOSAVE's session). Loading another project brings its own 64 kits. Keep a project's working kits here.
  • The kit library (128 kits and 128 single sounds) is a collection of its own: written to flash at once, and left as it is whichever project you load. Keep kits you want in every project here, such as your favorites or Hrtl's kit packs. A library kit can also be the morph target (LOAD → MORPH, From LIBRARY), and the sound browser on a track's sound pages offers single voices from its kits. To use a library kit with a pattern, load it and save it into a slot (SAVE → KIT, To SLOTS).

On the FM-1, turn PRESETS for the kit browser: it lists the factory kits (F1 …), then the library's (L1 …), then the slots. KNOB 1 jumps between them and OCT+ loads one. (CONFIG → BUTTONS & KITS, Kits first, puts the slots first.)

Importing LXR-02 kits: picking a folder takes the .SND files in it and in all its subfolders, nothing else: one project's folder (PROJ00 …), or an SD card's top folder for every project's kits, project by project. Chrome asks to "upload" the folder's files, but the page only reads them here and sends the kits to your FM-1: nothing leaves this computer. An LXR voice routed to the FX bus comes in as FX SEND 127 (the same sound); Elixir's own settings (sends, reverb, shaping, models) start at their defaults.

Kit library

In the library

On the FM-1, SAVE → KIT or MORPH with To LIBRARY saves a kit into it, SAVE → SOUND a single sound. Click a kit to download it as an LXR-02 .SND file. Back the library up before installing other firmware; a backup restores onto any Elixir, the emulator's too.

    Hrtl kit packs

    Two sets of 16 kits by Hrtl for the LXR-02, distributed with Erica Synths' permission. They go into the library's first empty slots (from L1, near the top of the kit browser's LIBRARY): a kit already there stays.

    Each pack takes about 12% of the kit library's flash, leaving the rest for your own kits and sounds. You can save your own kits over them at any time.

    Import LXR-02 kits into the library

    Up to 128 kits, starting at the library kit you choose; any that don't fit are left out (the page says how many). The LXR-02's factory kits are already built into Elixir, so there's no need to import them.

    Experimental: a phone emulator link into a kit

    Paste a link (or its text) from the phone emulator's Share. Its kit (the sound as it played, under its name) goes into a kit library slot on the connected FM-1, or downloads as a .ktlb for the library's Restore above. Only the kit comes across, not the pattern or WANDER. Check the kit after loading it.

    Empty the kit library

    Deletes every kit and sound in the library, for a clean start: neither installing V15 nor updating Elixir clears it. The factory kits are built into Elixir and stay; the kit slots and projects aren't touched.

    Kit slots

    The slots on the FM-1

    The playing project's 64 kits. Click one to download it as an LXR-02 .SND file (the LXR's parameters only: Elixir's own aren't in that format).

      Import LXR-02 kits into the slots

      Up to 64 kits, starting at the slot you choose, into the project that's playing. They replace the kits there; to keep them, save the project on the FM-1 (SAVE → PROJECT) or let AUTOSAVE keep them before you load another.

      Import an LXR-02 project

      Pick one project folder from an LXR-02 SD card (PROJ00 …), not the whole card. Its kits go into the kit slots by number (so each pattern still finds its kit), its patterns into Elixir's patterns and its songs into the songs. It replaces what's playing: save the project on the FM-1 afterwards. Elixir has 32 patterns to the LXR's 64, so pick which half.

      A song step that names a pattern from the other half plays pattern 32 instead.

      MIDI CC map

      Elixir takes the LXR-02's CC numbers. If your controller sends other numbers, point each one at the parameter you want here: any parameter Elixir takes by CC or NRPN, or nothing. Every change goes to the FM-1 as you make it.

      • Type into the box to search the parameters (a voice, "decay", "reverb", or a number such as CC 9 or NRPN 174). Empty means the LXR's own. Off makes the FM-1 ignore that CC.
      • A CC aimed at a short list (a waveform, a filter type, a model) sweeps the whole list over the knob's travel; the others take the value as it is.
      • Channels work as before: a CC for a voice's parameter is received on that voice's track channel or on the global channel; one without a voice, on any of Elixir's channels. CC 6, 98 and 99 stay NRPN's, and the REMOTE channels have their own map.
      • The map belongs to the FM-1, not a project: it stays whatever you load, and is saved with Elixir's settings a second after the sequencer stops.

      CCAs the LXRSets

      The CSV has a row per changed CC: cc,sets,name, for example 1,CC 9,D1 Coarse (the mod wheel sets D1's coarse pitch), 20,NRPN 174,D1 FX send or 9,off. The name column is only for reading. Loading a CSV replaces the whole map: the CCs it doesn't list go back to the LXR's.
